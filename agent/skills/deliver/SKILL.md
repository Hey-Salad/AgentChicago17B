# Deliver

Return ONE synthesized research brief:

- Title
- Executive summary (2–3 sentences)
- Reframed question
- Narrative (1–3 short paragraphs)
- Key findings (3–5)
- Open questions (2–4)
- Recommended next steps (2–4)
- Confidence: high | medium | low
- Bibliography with Chicago 17th notes (`[1]`, `[2]`, … matching real sources)

Prefer one coherent narrative over fragmented cards. Never invent URLs.

Then call `finalize_brief` to persist Markdown under `/workspace/briefs/`.
